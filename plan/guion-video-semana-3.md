## Guion (≈170 palabras, ≈75 segundos)

**[0–8 s] Gancho**
I'm an economist. I write code with AI. And this week I made a decision that most developers would call wrong.

**[8–25 s] Contexto**
I'm building Revisor App, bookkeeping for sole traders in Sweden. Receipts come in, you approve them, they get booked. Every week it tells the owner one thing: one truth, one next action.

**[25–50 s] La decisión**
The obvious feature was to let the AI give advice. "Spend less here, invoice sooner there." I said no. The model shows the numbers. It never tells you what to do. Why? Because I'm an economist, and I know what happens when an incentive sits inside the tool that's supposed to measure you. You stop trusting the measurement.

**[50–68 s] El error**
What went wrong: [una cosa concreta que falló esta semana: un bug, una suposición sobre los usuarios, un número mal calculado]. It cost me [X horas / X días]. I fixed it by [qué hiciste].

**[68–80 s] Cierre**
Next week I'm publishing the real numbers of Revisor App. Users, revenue in kronor, hours, costs. Even if they're small. Especially if they're small.
